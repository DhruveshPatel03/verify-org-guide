Change Personal → Organization (Google Play)

Why you usually can’t convert, and the practical alternatives.

Quick answer

You cannot convert an existing personal Google Play developer account into an organization account. The only reliable options are below.

Practical Options

  1. Create a new organization account: Register a new Google Play Developer account and choose Organization during signup. This requires paying the registration fee again and providing business docs.
  2. Use an existing organization account: Publish your app using a different account owned by your company or partner (with permission).
  3. Keep personal account: Complete the required testing (closed test with 12 testers for 14 days) and publish from your personal account.

How to create an Organization account

  1. Prepare your business documents (GST/PAN/company registration or equivalent).
  2. Sign up for a new Google account you’ll use for the organization (optional but recommended).
  3. Register for a new Google Play Developer account and select Organization.
  4. Pay the registration fee and complete business verification when prompted.
